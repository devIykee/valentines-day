# Housebets 30-second ad (HyperFrames project)

**Status: no submittable cut yet.** The first cut ("Thirty seconds") was withdrawn on 12 Nov and its render, its build script and the Hubie image it used have been removed from this branch. It broke three rules of the brief:

- it used illustrative screens with invented team names, where the brief requires real housebets.com screens;
- it was silent, where the brief asks for music, voice or both;
- its Hubie image came from another contestant's public repo, which fails the rights confirmation.

The recut is specified in `../03-AD-PROMPT-HYPERFRAMES.md`. It cannot be built until the inputs below exist.

## Drop the inputs here

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `recordings/` | Your own screen recordings of housebets.com, named as in the shot list in the prompt (`01-wallet-scan.mp4` … `06-withdraw.mp4`). |
| `assets/brand/` | Logo, Hubie art and anything else from the official starter pack. Nothing from other entrants. |
| `assets/audio/` | Music you hold the rights to, plus the licence or a screenshot of the plan that grants commercial use. |

The fonts in `assets/fonts/` (Figtree, Oxanium, Red Hat Display) are SIL OFL and fine to keep.
