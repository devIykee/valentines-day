## 2. Top 15 by max payout ÷ effort to scope

Effort is judged only from the published scope size. It is not a judgement of code quality.

1. **Squads v4 ($300k)**: one Solana program. Fixed dollar amounts, with no %-of-funds haircut.
2. **marginfi / Project 0 ($500k)**: one lending program. Critical has a $50k floor. Reply promised in 1 business day.
3. **Phoenix Legacy ($200k)**: one orderbook program plus a red-black tree library. 24h response.
4. **TON Core standard contracts ($100k)**: the in-scope contracts (wallets, jettons, multisig, nominators, DNS) are small and enumerated. The same $100k cap covers the node.
5. **Solana Mobile ($75k)**: two SKR programs (inflation and staking) plus Seed Vault. Narrow surface, but you burn SKR to submit.
6. **Across ($1M)**: high cap. Scope is wide (contracts, bots, front end) but sits in one repo. Attacks that need a privileged party are excluded.
7. **Frax ($10M cap)**: the highest ceiling found. Pay is 10% of exploitable funds, so realistic pay tracks per-contract TVL (~$392M across Frax listings). Only profitable theft pays.
8. **Hyperliquid (<$1M USDC)**: high cap. Scope is mainnet node/API logic errors and outages.
9. **Curve ($250k)**: proven payer (max paid in 2024). Large, heavily audited codebase.
10. **Ethereum Foundation ($1M)**: 10 clients plus compilers. A 2.0× Glamsterdam multiplier applies between Sepolia and Hoodi. Wide scope, but many targets.
11. **Agave (25,000 SOL ≈ $1.87M)**: the largest notional cap among L1 clients. Large codebase, rewards locked 12 months, SOL burn to submit.
12. **Trezor ($100k firmware, uncapped in exceptional cases)**: hardware threat model. Side channels need empirical proof.
13. **BNB Chain ($100k P1, P* case by case)**: published SLAs (P1 paid within 7 working days). Scope includes partner projects.
14. **Pacifica (25k USDC)**: a single perps venue with small TVL. Low cap, but KYC is required.
15. **Metaplex ($20k+ soft cap)**: tiered programs. Token Metadata and Core are the high tier.

## 3. Suspicious or weak terms

- **Tonkeeper/Keeper**: every tier starts at $0. Reporters waive all claims ("full compensation"). Keeper can claw back a bounty after disclosure and can end the program without notice.
- **Parity**: no amounts published, sole discretion. One forum account reports an NDA demanded before a $1k payout.
- **No amounts stated, sole discretion**: Jupiter, Chainflip (low/info explicitly unpaid), Ledger, Keystone, SaucerSwap, Blockscout.
- **Frax**: no dedicated security address (submit via DMs on social apps). No safe harbor. The token split is at the team's discretion. Submissions must include an "adversarial self-check" by an AI model.
- **Hyperliquid**: tiers are written as "<1M", which is an upper bound with no floor. Classification is at "sole discretion". Submissions become Hyper Foundation property.
- **Ethereum Foundation**: "experimental and discretionary", can be cancelled at any time, no explicit safe harbor.
- **BNB Chain**: discretionary, can decline any award. Bans AI-generated reports. Has a written 5-business-day dispute window, which is better than most.
- **Agave / Solana Mobile**: a non-refundable token burn per submission. Agave pays in 12-month-locked SOL. Solana Mobile pays in its own SKR token.
- **No safe-harbor language found**: Curve, Across (points to UMA terms, not checked), marginfi, Squads, Phoenix, BNB Chain, TON Core, Trezor, Flashbots, Pacifica, Metaplex, Braavos, Chainflip, SaucerSwap.
- **Disputes on record**: THORChain (above). Zcash's ZCG program was closed amid complaints about unpaid reports (Zcash is not in the table: Zebra runs no bounty).
