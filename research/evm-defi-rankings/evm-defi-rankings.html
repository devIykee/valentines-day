EVM DeFi Rankings

Top 1,000 DeFi protocols across every category, live on at least one EVM chain, ranked two ways. Data from DefiLlama, pulled 25 Oct 2026. Tap a name to visit the protocol.

List 1 · Daily fees

Fees are the only daily figure tracked across every DeFi category, so this ranks by 24h fees: what users paid the protocol that day. 4,452 EVM protocols qualified; these are the top 1,000, all with fee data. Trading volume is shown for DEXs, aggregators, bridges and options where tracked.

List 2 · Upgrades

Upgrade events = major versions shipped + published audit reports + upgrade milestones DefiLlama records. Ties broken by chains supported, then TVL. A rough proxy that leans on how many audits a protocol has published.

Scope

Left out as not-DeFi: exchanges, wallets, chains, oracles, frontends, games, NFT marketplaces, payments and centralized stablecoin issuers. Each protocol version is its own row. check marks fees that look high for the TVL. Filter by category below.
