EVM Vault Rankings

1,000 individual vaults on EVM chains, ranked two ways. Data from DefiLlama Yields, pulled 22 Oct 2026. Tap a vault to open its DefiLlama page.

List 1 · Daily yield

Vaults rarely have trading volume (DefiLlama tracks it for 60 of these). So this list ranks by daily yield paid: TVL × APY ÷ 365, the dollars the vault earns for depositors per day. 24h volume is shown where it exists.

List 2 · Upgrades

No source tracks upgrades per vault, so each vault takes its protocol's upgrade events: major versions + audit reports + upgrade milestones on DefiLlama. Vaults from the same protocol tie and are then ordered by TVL.

Scope

Vaults from yield aggregators, yield, curators, capital allocators, liquidity managers, leveraged farming, basis trading and options-vault protocols, plus Morpho and Euler vaults. Filtered to TVL ≥ $100K and APY ≤ 150%, excluding DefiLlama outliers. check marks APYs above 30%.

Source: yields.llama.fi/pools, joined with api.llama.fi/protocols for categories, audits and milestones. One vault on two chains appears as two rows.
