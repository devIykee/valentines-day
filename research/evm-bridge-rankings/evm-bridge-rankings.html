EVM Bridge Rankings

All 283 cross-chain bridges on DefiLlama that touch at least one EVM chain, ranked two ways. Pulled 22 Oct 2026. Tap a name to visit the bridge.

List 1 · Daily volume

Ranked by 24h bridged volume, which DefiLlama tracks for 120 of these. The rest follow, ordered by 24h fees where tracked, then by TVL (value locked in the bridge).

List 2 · Upgrades

Upgrade events = major versions shipped (V1→V2) + published audit reports + upgrade milestones DefiLlama records. Ties broken by number of chains supported, then TVL. Dead bridges still count their past upgrades (Multichain shut down in 2023).

Scope

Why not 1,000: DefiLlama lists about 365 bridges in total, and 283 touch an EVM chain. Includes generic bridges, cross-chain messaging, canonical L1↔L2 bridges and bridge aggregators. Use the type filter to narrow it down.

Sources: api.llama.fi/overview/bridges, /overview/bridge-aggregators, /overview/fees, /protocols. The dedicated bridges API is now paid, so per-route flows aren't included.
