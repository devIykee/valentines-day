EVM Yield Protocol Rankings

1,000 yield protocols on EVM chains, ranked two ways. Data from DefiLlama, pulled 22 Oct 2026. Tap a column header to see the method; tap a name to visit the protocol.

List 1 · Daily activity

Yield protocols don't have “trading volume” the way DEXs do, and DefiLlama tracks volume for only 12 of these. So this list ranks by 24h fees: the money users actually paid through the protocol in a day. 351 have fee data; the other 649 follow, ordered by TVL. 24h volume is shown where it exists.

List 2 · Upgrades

Upgrade events = major versions shipped (V1→V2→V3…) + published audit reports (each usually covers a new release) + upgrade milestones DefiLlama records (launches, migrations, new vaults). Ties broken by EVM chain deployments, then TVL. GitHub commit history wasn't reachable from this session.

Scope

Categories: lending, yield, yield aggregators, liquid staking & restaking, staking pools, farms, leveraged farming, basis trading, options vaults, curators, capital allocators, liquidity managers. Must be live on at least one EVM chain with TVL above zero. check marks fee figures that look implausibly high for the TVL.

Sources: api.llama.fi/protocols, /overview/fees, /overview/dexs, /overview/aggregators, /overview/options. Separate versions (e.g. Aave V2, Aave V3) are listed separately, as DefiLlama does.
