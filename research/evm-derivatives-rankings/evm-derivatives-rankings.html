EVM Derivatives Rankings

All 353 derivatives protocols on DefiLlama that run on an EVM chain: perps, options, synthetics and rate derivatives, ranked two ways. Pulled 23 Oct 2026.

List 1 · Daily activity

DefiLlama moved derivatives volume behind its paid plan. Trading fees scale directly with volume, so this ranks by 24h fees (91 tracked), then open interest, then TVL. Open interest (OI) is the value of open positions, shown for 53.

List 2 · Upgrades

Upgrade events = major versions + published audit reports + upgrade milestones DefiLlama records. Ties broken by chains supported, then TVL.

Scope

Off-chain and non-EVM venues are excluded (Lighter main, Aster, edgeX, Paradex, Pacifica). Hyperliquid is included because HyperEVM shares its chain, though its perps engine itself isn't EVM. Only 353 qualify, so this list isn't 1,000 long.

Sources: api.llama.fi/overview/fees, /overview/open-interest, /protocols.
