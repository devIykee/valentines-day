EVM Gaming Rankings

All 65 gaming protocols on DefiLlama that run on an EVM chain and have activity data, ranked two ways. Pulled 22 Oct 2026. Tap a name to visit the game.

List 1 · Daily activity

Ranked by 24h fees: what players paid the game or platform that day. Only 7 of these have trading volume on DefiLlama, so fees are the better measure. 31 have fee data; the rest follow by TVL.

List 2 · Upgrades

Upgrade events = major versions + published audits + upgrade milestones DefiLlama records. Few games publish audits, so most score 0 or 1 and the order below the top few is mostly tie-breaks (chains, then TVL).

Coverage gap

DefiLlama tracks DeFi money flows, not game economies. Most big web3 games aren't listed at all, and listed ones like Axie only show on-chain fees. For player counts and in-game spend, sources like DappRadar are better.
